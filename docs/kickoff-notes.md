# Kickoff notes: issues in the brief and how we resolved them

The [kickoff brief](kickoff.md) is the source of truth for scope and is kept exactly as received. While
turning it into a backlog and ADRs we found a few inconsistencies and gaps. This page records each one
and the resolution the kickoff sprint adopted, so nobody has to rediscover them.

## Defaults the kickoff adopted

Summarised from [open questions](open-questions.md): launch mix is sarees, fabric by the metre, and
stoles and dupattas; Razorpay stays in test mode; COD limit ₹5,000; 7-day returns; founding year
unconfirmed, so the Heritage-seal logo concept is on hold.
